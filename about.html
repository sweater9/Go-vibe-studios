About GoVibe Mix

GoVibe Mix is a free set of music and editing tools that run in your web browser. It is made for music lovers, content creators, DJs, music makers, and audio and video editors.

What you can do here

DJ Console: up to four decks, EQ, filters, an effect unit, loops and loop rolls, eight hot cues, beat grid tools, tempo sync, beat jumps up to 8 beats, Automix with ordering by key and tempo, a mix maker that renders your queue into one beatmatched WAV or MP3 with a tracklist, a beat maker, a library of your tracks with playlists (importable from rekordbox, Traktor and Serato), MIDI controller mapping and mix recording. Audio Editor (now with background noise removal that runs on your device): trim, cut, fade, normalize, change speed, volume and pitch, use a 3 band EQ and a noise gate, and export WAV or MP3. Video Editor: join and trim clips, change speed with ramps, grade colour, zoom and pan, key out a green screen, stabilise shaky footage, add styled and animated text and subtitles, record your screen, lay music under it with ducking, and export in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1 at 480p to 1080p. BPM and key finder: tempo and Camelot key for your tracks, plus tap tempo. Audio converter: MP3 or WAV, one file or many. Multitrack Studio: audio, keys and drum tracks on a timeline with a piano roll, loop, a mixer with effects (EQ, compressor, saturation, chorus, delay, reverb), sends and a master chain, markers, time signatures from 2/4 to 12/8, recording straight onto a track, MIDI import and export, and WAV, MP3 or stems export. Voice recorder: several takes from your microphone. Mastering: LUFS and true peak meters, streaming loudness targets, limiter and A/B compare. Loop maker: royalty free drum, bass, chord and melody loops in your key and tempo. Key and scale helper: scales, chords in a key, DJ compatible keys and a scale finder. Chord and fretboard tools: a chord finder, guitar and ukulele shapes, a fretboard scale map, the circle of fifths and a chord chart transposer. Looper: record a loop and layer overdubs on top. Audio: drop in a file and let Auto listen and fix it, pick a job and steps in Guided, or open the full editor in Pro, with loudness before and after. Silence remover, Stereo to mono converter, Tone generator and Video to audio converter: small single jobs. Slowed and reverb maker: slowed, sped up or nightcore versions with reverb. Pitch and speed changer: change key or tempo on their own and loop a section to practise. Audio cutter and joiner: trim, cut out a section, fade, or join files. Volume and bass booster: louder, more bass, normalize, with a live preview. Music visualizer: turn a song into an MP4 video with moving bars, a circle or a waveform, your cover art and a title, in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1. Metronome: tap tempo, accents, subdivisions, swing and a tempo trainer. The Audio Editor and Voice recorder also have a spectrogram that shows pitch and noise at a glance. Learn music and production: eight lessons with an ear trainer and quizzes. Learn to DJ: short DJ lessons with an ear trainer.

Why it works the way it does

Your files stay on your device. The tools load your audio and video into your browser and process them there, so there is no upload, no account and no waiting in a queue. Closing the tab ends the session, so export what you want to keep.

What is free and what is not

Every tool on the site is free to use.

Honest limits

Detection of tempo and musical key is an estimate, and it works best on clear, steady music. Stem splitting is a beta feature based on signal processing, not artificial intelligence, so expect leftovers of other instruments in each stem. Video export records the preview in real time, so a one minute video takes about a minute. Browsers differ, and Chrome and Edge give the best results.
