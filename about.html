About GoVibe Mix

GoVibe Mix is a free set of music and editing tools that run in your web browser. It is made for music lovers, content creators, DJs, music makers, and audio and video editors.

In short

Think of it as a studio, a DJ booth and an editing desk that open in a browser tab. Mix tracks with an Automix that reads each song's intro, drop and outro. Make a longer DJ edit or a shorter radio edit of a track. Record, tune, cut, slow down, master and convert audio. Cut and edit video. Learn with short lessons. There is nothing to install, no account to make, and nothing is uploaded. It works offline once you have opened it.

What you can do here

Mix and DJ

  • DJ Console: up to four decks, EQ, filters, an effect unit, loops and loop rolls, eight hot cues, beat grid tools, tempo sync, beat jumps, MIDI controller mapping and mix recording.
  • Automix: reads each track's tempo, key, energy, intro, drop and outro, picks a transition for every pair and blends them on a phrase. Press "Try it with demo tracks" to hear it with no files, and use the set map to see how your set rises and falls.
  • Mix maker: renders your queue into one beatmatched WAV or MP3 with a tracklist.
  • DJ edit maker: a longer DJ edit or a shorter radio edit of a track.
  • Library: your tracks with playlists, importable from rekordbox, Traktor and Serato, and exportable as M3U, CSV or rekordbox XML.
  • BPM and key finder, Learn to DJ.

Make music

  • Multitrack Studio: audio, keys and drum tracks on a timeline with a piano roll, a mixer with effects, sends and a master chain, markers, time signatures from 2/4 to 12/8, recording straight onto a track, MIDI import and export, and WAV, MP3 or stems export.
  • Loop maker, Looper, Voice recorder, Metronome.
  • Key and scale helper, Chord and fretboard tools, Learn music and production.

Edit and fix audio

  • Audio Editor: trim, cut, fade, normalize, change speed, volume and pitch, a 3 band EQ, a noise gate, background noise removal that runs on your device, and a spectrogram. Exports WAV or MP3.
  • Audio: drop in a file and let Auto listen and fix it, pick a job in Guided, or open the full editor in Pro, with loudness before and after.
  • Mastering: LUFS and true peak meters, streaming loudness targets, a limiter and A/B compare.
  • Slowed and reverb maker, Pitch and speed changer, Audio cutter and joiner, Volume and bass booster, Silence remover, Stereo to mono converter, Audio converter, Tone generator.

Video

  • Video Editor: join and trim clips, speed ramps, colour grading, zoom and pan, green screen, stabilising, styled and animated text and subtitles, screen recording, music with ducking, and export in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1 at 480p to 1080p.
  • Music visualizer: turn a song into an MP4 with moving bars, a circle or a waveform, your cover art and a title.
  • Video to audio converter.

Look and feel

Choose Dark neon, Light or High contrast with the Theme button in the menu. Your choice is remembered on your device.

Why it works the way it does

Your files stay on your device. The tools load your audio and video into your browser and process them there, so there is no upload, no account and no waiting in a queue. Closing the tab ends the session, so export what you want to keep.

What is free and what is not

Every tool on the site is free to use.

Honest limits

Detection of tempo and musical key is an estimate, and it works best on clear, steady music. Stem splitting is a beta feature based on signal processing, not artificial intelligence, so expect leftovers of other instruments in each stem. Video export records the preview in real time, so a one minute video takes about a minute. Browsers differ, and Chrome and Edge give the best results.

Who makes it

GoVibe Mix is an independent project. It has no ads and no data collection. It stays free because nothing runs on a server: your own device does the work.

GoVibe Mix (govibemix.com) is part of GoVibe Tools. It is a different website from other products and mixtape services with a similar name, such as Govibe Video & Audio, and it is not connected to them.
