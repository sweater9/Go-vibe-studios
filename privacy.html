Privacy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 (DJ library added)

Go Vibe Studios is a set of tools that run in your browser. We have no accounts, no database and no analytics, and your audio and video files are never uploaded to us.

Your files

Audio, video and music you open in the editors, the DJ mixer or the video editor are processed on your own device. They are not sent to this site.

What is stored in your browser

  • Your Replicate API key, if you add one on the Create page. It is kept in this browser only: in local storage if you tick "Remember on this device", otherwise for the current tab only. "Remove key" deletes it.
  • Proxy address (optional), games you add on the Play page, and your lesson progress.
  • Your DJ library, if you add tracks or load them into a deck with "Remember tracks" ticked: the audio files and their BPM, key, beat grid and cue points are kept in this browser's storage (IndexedDB). Use the ✕ button in the Library to remove a track, or clear the site's data in your browser. MIDI mappings and saved beats are also kept here.
  • A temporary copy of a generated track, used to pass it from the Create page to the Audio Editor. It is deleted once the editor has loaded it.
  • Cached copies of the site's own files, so the site can open offline.

You can clear all of this from your browser's site settings. This site does not set cookies of its own.

When data leaves your device

  • AI generation (Create page): your prompt and API key are sent to Replicate, or to the proxy address you entered, which forwards them to Replicate. Replicate's own terms and privacy policy apply, and Replicate bills the account that owns the key. A proxy run from this project's worker/ folder does not store or log keys, but a proxy run by anyone else is under their control, so only use one you trust.
  • Embedded games (Play page): a game you add is a third-party page shown inside this site. It may collect data under its own policy. Only add games you trust.
  • Hosting: the site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard web-server data such as IP addresses and the pages requested, under its own privacy policy.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: open an issue at github.com/sweater9/go-vibe-studios.
