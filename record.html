Voice and mic recorder

Record your voice or an instrument with your microphone, keep several takes, then download WAV or MP3 or open a take in the editor. Recordings stay on your device.

Start

Your browser will ask to use the microphone. Nothing is recorded until you press Record, and nothing is uploaded.

    Tips

    • Wear headphones if you turn on "Hear myself", or the speakers will feed back into the microphone.
    • Aim for a level that peaks in the green or yellow. If the bar turns red, move back or lower the input volume in your system settings.
    • Record a few seconds of silence first. It helps the noise gate in the Audio Editor find the noise level.
    • Press the space bar to start and stop when no button is focused.
    • Recordings are held in memory until you close the page, so download the takes you want to keep.
