How it works

  • Recording turns off the browser's echo, noise and level tricks, so you get your clean voice. The take is shifted to make up for the delay of your device, and you can fine tune the timing in milliseconds.
  • Pitch correction listens to each note, then moves it to the nearest note of the scale you pick. It does not change the timing of notes.
  • The vocal sound is a low cut, presence and air, a compressor, reverb, echo and a doubler. All of it runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
