How it works

  • Each note has two oscillators, a sub oscillator and noise (or your sample) going through a filter and an amp. Both have their own envelope, and an LFO can move the pitch, filter or volume.
  • Saving renders the notes again offline with the same sound engine, so the file matches what you heard.
  • Your sounds, samples and recordings never leave your device. MIDI keyboards are read by your browser and only used here.
