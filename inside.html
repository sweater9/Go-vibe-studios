What's inside

Every tool on GoVibe Mix, and where it runs. Your files are opened and processed on your own device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no uploads.

Runs on your device

  • Multitrack Studio: Record, arrange and mix audio, keys and drum tracks on a timeline. Piano roll, loops, markers, time signatures, MIDI, a shared mixer with effects, WAV and MP3 export.
  • Loop maker: Drum, bass, chord and melody loops in your key and tempo. WAV, MIDI, or open in the Studio.
  • Voice recorder: Record your voice or an instrument, keep several takes, download WAV or MP3.
  • Slowed and reverb maker: Tape style speed and pitch with a generated reverb, previewed live and rendered offline.
  • Pitch and speed changer: WSOLA time stretch and pitch shift with a loop, the same method as the Audio Editor.
  • Audio cutter and joiner: Trim, cut out, fade and join, saved as WAV or MP3.
  • Volume and bass booster: Gain, shelf filters, soft clipping and peak normalize, rendered offline.
  • Music visualizer: Draws your song with its own FFT analysis and exports an MP4 or WebM with the audio using WebCodecs, with a real time recording fallback.
  • Metronome: Tap tempo, accents, time signatures, subdivisions, swing and a tempo trainer.
  • Looper: Record a loop in time, layer overdubs, mute and mix the layers, and download the result.
  • Chord and fretboard tools: Name a chord from its notes, see guitar and ukulele shapes, map a scale on the neck, explore the circle of fifths and transpose chord charts.
  • Key and scale helper: Scales, chords in a key, DJ compatible keys and a scale finder on a playable keyboard.
  • Audio Editor: Trim, cut, fade, change speed and pitch, EQ, noise gate, rumble filter and background noise removal. Export WAV or MP3. The noise removal engine ships with the site, so nothing is downloaded.
  • Video Editor: Join and trim clips, speed changes, colour grading, zoom and pan, green screen, stabilise, titles and subtitles (import and export SRT), screen, camera and voiceover recording, crop, rotate and flip, pictures, logos and stickers, a silence remover, background noise removal for clip sound, drawing on the picture, music with automatic ducking, and fast or real time export up to 4K, or as a GIF.
  • Mastering: Measure LUFS and true peak, match streaming loudness, limit, compare and export.
  • Audio converter: Convert audio to MP3 or WAV, one file or many, then download a ZIP.
  • DJ Console: Up to four decks, EQ, filters, an effect unit, loops and loop rolls, eight hot cues, beat grid tools, tempo sync, beat jumps up to 8 beats, Automix with ordering by key and tempo, a mix maker that renders your queue into one beatmatched WAV or MP3 with a tracklist, a beat maker, playlists, MIDI controller mapping and mix recording. Stem splitting here is signal processing, not artificial intelligence.
  • BPM and key finder: Find the tempo and Camelot key of your tracks, plus a tap tempo counter.
  • Learn to DJ: Ten short lessons, a beatmatching trainer, phrase and key trainers.
  • Learn music and production: Rhythm, scales, chords, mixing and mastering with an ear trainer and quizzes.

These tools work offline once the site has loaded, and you can install it as an app. Closing the tab ends the session, so export what you want to keep.

Nothing leaves your device

Every tool on this page works on your device. We removed the one tool that sent a text prompt to an outside service, so no tool on this site sends your files or your input anywhere.

What we left out on purpose

There are no cloud artificial intelligence features and no model downloads from other sites, because they would mean sending your audio or video away or fetching files from a third party. Noise removal uses a small engine that is part of this site.

Check it yourself

Open your browser's developer tools, choose the Network tab, and use any tool above. You will see requests only to this site. The code is open source on GitHub, and the open source licences of the parts we include are listed in the repository.
