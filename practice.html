How it works

  • Speed and key are changed with a time stretching method, so slowing a song down does not lower its pitch. Large changes can add a slight warble.
  • The parts are separated with signal processing, not AI. The vocals are found as the centred voice, the drums by their sharp hits, and the bass by its low frequencies, so there is some bleed between them.
  • Your song is never uploaded. It is processed in your browser.
