What the numbers mean

  • LUFS measures how loud music seems, using the ITU BS.1770 method with gating. Streaming services turn loud tracks down to about −14 LUFS, so a track mastered far louder does not play louder, it only loses punch.
  • True peak catches the peaks between samples that can distort after MP3 or AAC conversion. A ceiling of −1 dBTP is the usual safe choice.
  • The limiter looks 2 ms ahead, so it stops peaks without clipping. The tool raises the level until the loudness target is reached, within the ceiling.
  • Hitting a very loud target on dynamic music means heavy limiting. Watch the reduction figure. Past about 6 dB the sound starts to flatten.
