Loop and sample maker

Make drum loops, basslines, chord loops and melodies in the key and tempo you choose. Every sound is generated in your browser, so there are no samples to license: use the results in your own music. Download as WAV or MIDI, or open one as an editable track in the Studio.

Settings

Pick a loop and press Play. Loops repeat until you stop them.

About these loops

  • Drums, bass, chords and melodies are made by the same built in instruments as the Studio. They are simple on purpose, a starting point for you to build on.
  • The tail of each loop is folded back onto its start so it repeats without a click.
  • Use "Open in Studio" to get the notes as an editable track, then change them in the piano roll.
  • Not sure which key? Use the Key and scale helper. Want to know a track's key? Try the BPM and key finder.
