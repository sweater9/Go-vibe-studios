Rhythm trainer

Practise your timing. A click plays, you tap a rhythm on the big button or any key, and you see how early or late each tap was, how steady you are and whether you speed up or slow down. It is a practice tool with no scores. Everything stays on your device.

Tap the pattern you chose, not the click. For example, with "The off beats" you tap between the clicks.

Device delay

Screens, keyboards and Bluetooth add a small delay. Press Calibrate and tap on eight clicks, and your taps are shifted to cancel it out.

What to try

    How it works

    • The click is scheduled with the audio clock, so it does not wobble. Each tap is timed against the same clock and matched to the nearest note of the pattern.
    • Average timing says whether you tend to play early or late. Spread says how steady you are, and spread matters most for playing that sounds tight. Drift shows whether you speed up or slow down.
    • Dropping the click for two bars out of every four, or after the count in, tests your inner pulse.
    • Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Only the timing of your taps is measured.
