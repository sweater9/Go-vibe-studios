Tempo converter

Type a tempo, or tap it, and get the length of every note in milliseconds and the matching rate in Hz. Use it to set delay times, reverb pre delay, LFO speeds and compressor release so effects sit in time with the song. Everything runs in your browser.

NoteStraight (ms)Dotted (ms)Triplet (ms)Straight (Hz)

Hz is how many times per second the note repeats, which is the rate to use for an LFO or tremolo. Dotted notes are one and a half times as long, triplets are two thirds as long.

Loop length calculator

Work out how long a loop lasts at the tempo above, or find the tempo of a loop you already know the length of. Beats per bar is 4 for most pop, dance and hip hop.
