Tips

  • Play one note at a time and let it ring. The needle settles after about half a second.
  • Pluck a string firmly, then tune up to the note from below. Strings hold their tuning best that way.
  • The green zone means within 5 cents, which is close enough that most ears cannot tell the difference.
  • Choose an instrument and press a string name to hear a reference note, then match it by ear or with the needle.
  • Most orchestras tune to A4 = 440 Hz. Some baroque groups use 415 Hz and some European orchestras use 442 Hz.
  • Want to see what else is in your sound? Open the Voice recorder for a live spectrogram.
