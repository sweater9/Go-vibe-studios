    Tips

    • The first loop sets the length. Everything you add after it repeats in time with it.
    • Press Overdub at any moment. The new layer starts at the top of the next pass and runs for one full loop.
    • Start with a rhythm part such as a groove or chord strum, then add bass, melody and harmony.
    • Wear headphones, or the loop will feed back into the microphone and get louder with every layer.
    • Happy with it? Download the mix, or open it in the Audio Editor or the Studio to build a full track.
