Tips

  • Press the space bar to start and stop when no button is focused. The up and down arrows change the tempo by one.
  • Tap the Tap tempo button along with a song to find its speed. For a full track, the BPM and key finder measures it for you.
  • Practise a hard passage at 60 percent of the target tempo, then raise it in small steps. The trainer does this for you.
  • Turn on a subdivision to feel the spaces between beats. Triplets are useful for shuffles and blues.
  • The tempo marking under the number (Largo, Allegro and so on) is only a guide to the feel.
