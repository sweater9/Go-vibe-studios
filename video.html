Fast export builds the video frame by frame instead of waiting for it to play, which is often quicker than real time on a good computer (it depends on your device and the footage). It needs a recent Chrome or Edge and clips at normal speed. Otherwise the editor plays the video through in real time while it records, so a 1-minute video takes about a minute. Keep this tab open and visible while it runs. The file is MP4 if your browser can make it, otherwise WebM.
